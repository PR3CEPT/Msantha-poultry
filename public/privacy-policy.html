1. Data Safety & Zero-Telemetry Architecture

Msantha Poultry Farm Manager is built upon a 100% offline-first architecture. All records—including flock inventories, egg production statistics, commercial customer incubation logs, cashbook income/expenses, and audit trails—are stored strictly within local sandbox memory (IndexedDB and LocalStorage) on your physical Android smartphone or device.

🛡️ No Remote Server Sync

No agricultural data, sales transactions, or financial figures are automatically uploaded to external cloud servers, advertisers, or analytics brokers.

🔒 Local Device Sandboxing

Database storage is isolated within Android's protected application sandbox, accessible only by authorized staff with valid security PIN credentials.

2. Android Permissions Transparency

In accordance with Android 14 & 15 Scoped Storage and Google Play Store Developer Policies, this application minimizes system access:

  • No Location Access: We do not track or collect GPS or cell tower locations.
  • No Camera or Microphone Access: The app does not access your camera, microphone, or media hardware.
  • No Contact List Access: The app does not access your personal phone contacts. Customer telephone numbers entered for incubation pickup alerts are stored only within the local farm database.
  • Network Access (Internet): Used only to fetch software updates and transmit WhatsApp notification intents when explicitly initiated by the user.
  • Scoped File Storage: Used strictly when the user chooses to download printable PDF/HTML receipts or export full farm database backups (.json) to device storage.

3. Customer Information & Communications

When operating commercial incubation services, operators may enter customer names, phone numbers, and villages to generate collection receipts and trigger WhatsApp hatch notifications. This information is confidential to Msantha Poultry Farm and is never sold, shared, or repurposed.

4. Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) & PIN Security

To protect sensitive farm financial data, the system incorporates Role-Based Access Control:

  • Managing Director & Sales & Distribution Manager: Full operational and financial ledger authority.
  • Operations & View-Only Staff: Restricted to recording day-to-day egg counts and flock observations without financial or stock deletion permissions.
  • Quick Session Lock: Operators can lock the screen at any time to require PIN re-authentication.

5. Compliance with Malawi & International Laws

This policy complies with the Malawi Data Protection Act (DPA 2024), the African Union Convention on Cyber Security and Personal Data Protection, and international GDPR principles regarding data minimization, purpose limitation, and storage limitation.
